Changes in Your Hot Water Heater

Have you observed any modifications currently to your water heater that indicate you may need to repair or update it? Although smaller problems might not seem really worth repairing, they are able to lead to huge issues if left untreated.
A few examples of problems which indicate a repair is necessary include:
  • Loss of water pressure.
  • Less hot water than normal.
  • Water never gets truly hot.
  • Foul smelling or dirty water.
