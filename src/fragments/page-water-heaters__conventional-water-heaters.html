Conventional Water Heaters

Conventional water heaters are what you’ll traditionally find in most homes. They characteristic a big tank wherein water is saved and continually heated to ensure that there is constantly a supply of hot water on-hand.

The pros of conventional water heaters include:

  • Holds large amounts of water
  • Suitable for larger families
  • Low-maintenance
  • Long lifespan

The cons of conventional water heaters include:

  • May waste energy
  • Not suitable for small homes
  • Expensive to replace
  • Takes up a lot of space
