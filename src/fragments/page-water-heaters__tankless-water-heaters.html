Tankless Water Heaters

Unlike conventional water heaters, tankless water heaters are relatively new to the marketplace. Tankless models improve on some areas of the conventional models, but still leave some things to be desired. For some, a tankless model is an energy-efficient choice that can save money in the long run.

However, these are not for everyone or every type of home. The pros and cons include:

The pros of Tankless water heaters include:
  • Uses less energy
  • Heats water on-demand
  • Takes up less space
  • Low-maintenance
The cons of Tankless water heaters include:
  • Expensive to install & retrofit
  • Multiple units needed for larger homes
